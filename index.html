About this dApp

This is a blockchain-based voting system for Pakistan, built as the working version of my Assignment 1 idea. In many elections people doubt the final count, because paper forms can be lost or changed. Here every vote is recorded on the Sepolia blockchain, so nobody can secretly change it and anyone can check the result.

This page talks to a Solidity smart contract. Connect MetaMask, view candidates, and cast one vote per wallet.

  • Owner adds candidates
  • Every wallet can vote only once
  • Results are stored on the blockchain and cannot be secretly changed
  • No real name or CNIC is stored, only the wallet address
